<h2><span class="thin-text">Pipe sheet piles</span> from Eco-Tech</h2>
<p>These robust products are designed for a long service life and effectively prevent soil movement. Our range includes pipe sheet piles in different weights and dimensions, making it easy to select products that match the needs of your project.</p>
<p class="mb-80">COMBIWALL pipe sheet piles are steel sheet-pile walls designed to retain differences in ground level. Their structure consists of rolled sections fitted with interlocks at the edges. COMBIWALL pipe sheet piles can be installed by pressing, driving or vibratory driving.</p>

<div class="wp-block-columns"><div class="wp-block-column is-vertically-aligned-center">
<h2><span class="thin-text">Applications of</span> pipe sheet piles</h2>
<hr class="et-line-yellow">
<p>Pipe sheet piles are often used to build partitions, breakwaters, cofferdams and barriers to groundwater flow. They can also form excavation support for foundations, basements, car parks and pumping stations.</p>
<p>COMBIWALL pipe sheet piles can be used for cantilever or ground-anchored retaining walls, railway retaining walls, foundation formwork, bridge abutments and tunnel-wall structures. They are also useful for emergency flood-embankment repairs.</p>

<h2><span class="thin-text">Installation of</span> Combiwall pipe sheet piles</h2>
<p>Installation is straightforward. Positioning the piles successively along the perimeter of an excavation or breakwater creates permanent support or a watertight temporary wall that reduces groundwater inflow. Where required, an anchor or additional internal bracing can be installed to transfer horizontal loads.</p>
