<div class="wp-block-columns"><div class="wp-block-column"><h2><span class="thin-text">A broad range of</span><br>steel pipe insulation</h2><hr class="et-line-yellow"><p>We offer a complete range of insulation systems for <strong><a href="/steel-pipes/">steel pipes</a></strong> with diameters from 159 to 2,400 mm. Steel pipe insulation improves resistance to corrosion, mechanical damage and chemical or temperature exposure.</p><p>We insulate steel pipes from 159 to 2,400 mm in diameter and from 6 to 16 metres in length. Both <strong><a href="/steel-pipes-seamless/">seamless steel pipes</a></strong> and <strong><a href="/steel-pipes-longitudinally-welded/">welded steel pipes</a></strong> can be insulated.</p></div><div class="wp-block-column"><figure class="wp-block-image size-full"><a href="/img/posts/2022/06/izolowanie-rur-stalowych-3-840x480-1.jpeg"><img src="/img/posts/2022/06/izolowanie-rur-stalowych-3-840x480-1.jpeg" alt="Insulated steel pipe" class="wp-image-15386"/></a></figure></div></div>

<h2><span class="thin-text">Correctly selected and applied</span> pipe insulation provides:</h2><hr class="et-line-yellow"><div class="wp-block-columns"><div class="wp-block-column"><ul><li>high resistance to mechanical damage;</li><li>resistance to chemicals, moisture, electrical breakdown, elevated temperatures and UV radiation;</li><li>high adhesion to the pipe surface.</li></ul></div><div class="wp-block-column"><ul><li>environmental neutrality;</li><li>around 50 years of anti-corrosion protection for underground pipelines;</li><li>complete protection of steel-pipe welds;</li><li>the ability to join insulated pipes without damaging the coating;</li><li>low specific transition resistance for gas pipelines.</li></ul></div></div>
